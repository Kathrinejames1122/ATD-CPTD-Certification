A dedicated study guide and talent development reference for professionals preparing for the **ATD Certified Professional in Talent Development (CPTD) Certification Exam**, covering personal capabilities, professional development practices, and organizational impact.

---

## Exam Overview

The Certified Professional in Talent Development (CPTD) certification validates the ability to apply talent development knowledge, make professional decisions, and develop solutions that support individual and organizational performance.

- **Exam Format**: Computer-based examination combining multiple-choice questions and case-management questions; available at authorized testing centers or through remote proctoring.
- **Target Audience**: Experienced talent development professionals, learning and development specialists, instructional designers, organizational development practitioners, and talent development managers.
- **Prerequisites**: Candidates must meet one eligibility pathway: five years of relevant experience plus 60 professional development hours; four years of experience with a current APTD credential; or five years of experience plus completion of an ATD Master program within the past five years.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Building Personal Capability

- Communication strategies, emotional intelligence, decision-making, and professional relationships.
- Collaboration, leadership, cultural awareness, inclusion, and conflict management.
- Project management, compliance, ethical behavior, and responsible information use.

### 2. Developing Professional Capability

- Learning sciences, adult learning theories, instructional design, and learning outcome development.
- Training delivery, facilitation, learning technologies, and knowledge management.
- Career and leadership development, coaching, and evaluation of learning impact.

### 3. Impacting Organizational Capability

- Business insight, consulting, business partnering, and organizational development.
- Organizational culture, talent strategy, performance improvement, and change management.
- Data and analytics, future readiness, innovation, and alignment of talent development with business objectives.
